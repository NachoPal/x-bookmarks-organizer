    Connect your X account

    The app reads your bookmarks straight from X. Authorizing it opens X's consent page once; every later sync runs on its own.

    Choose how bookmarks are sorted

    A first sync designs a category tree, then files every bookmark into it. You can change any of this later in Settings.

    Run the first sync

    This fetches your bookmarks from X and categorizes them. A large library can take a few minutes - you can keep using the app while it runs.

    Reset your library?

    This deletes every bookmark, category, summary and score stored on this computer, and the next sync pulls everything again from the start.

    Nothing is removed from X, and your X sign-in and categorization settings are kept.

    Start a paid ranking run?

    Each bookmark is scored once for learning value, and the score is what the Top score order reads. Your bookmarks, categories and summaries are not modified.

    You can close the app while it runs; a bookmark that has already been scored is never paid for twice.

    Move to a category

    Pick one category. The post is filed there and removed from the categories it is in now.

    Edit categories

    Add a category anywhere in the tree, or delete one. Deleting a category also deletes its sub-categories, and permanently deletes any post left filed nowhere else.
